Common questions

How do I drive?

Tap Drive on the home screen. The left stick drives, the right stick turns, and the buttons on the right run the robot's mechanisms — claw, lift, intake. Everything about those controls can be changed under Settings → Controls: size, opacity, where each button sits, what it does, and whether a mechanism is a one-button toggle or a two-button hold.

Can I drive my own team's robot?

Yes. Go to Settings → Account → Submit a Robot, fill in your team and email, and choose your robot's CAD file. We rebuild it by hand into a drivable robot — real joints, real pivots — and email you when it is ready. It usually takes a few days.

Send a .step or .f3d if you can. Those keep the exact surfaces, are far smaller than a mesh export, and let us pick the right level of detail on our end. A .fbx, .urdf or .zip also works if that is all you have.

How do I get my file into the app?

Open the Files app on your device, find the RoboSimL folder under On My iPhone or On My iPad, and copy your CAD file into it. It will then appear when you tap Choose File.

What is a robot code?

When we finish setting up a robot that was submitted privately, we send the team a short code. Entering it under Settings → Account adds that robot to your picker. Codes are shareable — pass one to a teammate and it works on their device too.

You do not need a code to use the app. Every robot listed publicly is drivable without one.

I reinstalled and lost my code

Email us from the address you submitted with and we will send it again.

My robot could not be set up

It happens, and we always say why. The usual causes are an export that came through as one welded piece with nothing to pivot, an archive missing its meshes, or an assembly that arrived incomplete. All of them are fixed by re-exporting — we will tell you exactly what to change.

Delete my data

Email us and we will delete your submission, your email address, and any message addressed to you. There is no account to close — the app does not have accounts. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.

Privacy

The app collects nothing unless you choose to submit a robot. No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The full details are in the privacy policy.
